## 1. Group A Benchmark Entities

### Atomic Item: OmniRetail (Nigeria / West Africa)
- Profitable B2B FMCG asset-light network ranked #1 fastest growing in Africa.
  > "OmniRetail achieved audited operating profitability (EBITDA positive) across Nigeria and Ghana while holding zero warehouses and zero delivery trucks, processing over $500M annualized GMV."
  [[benchmark-omniretail-nigeria]]
- Anti-disintermediation through software and payments.
  > "By bundling Mplify distributor ERP and OmniPay embedded wallet with 14-day credit lines, OmniRetail achieved a 96% repeat order rate with negligible offline platform leakage."
  [[benchmark-omniretail-nigeria]]

### Atomic Item: Cartona (Egypt)
- Asset-light Egyptian marketplace operating in severe currency devaluation.
  > "Cartona expanded to over 188,000 retailers and 3,000 wholesalers across Egypt, achieving contribution margin positivity and nearing consolidated EBITDA break-even through a 100% asset-light model."
  [[benchmark-cartona-egypt]]
- Phygital sales force migration to night-time self-serve ordering.
  > "Over 78% of repeat supermarket orders on Cartona are placed autonomously by shop owners between 9:00 PM and midnight, reducing field rep cost per order by 72%."
  [[benchmark-cartona-egypt]]

### Atomic Item: Bazaar Technologies (Pakistan)
- Transition from 1P logistics burn to asset-light merchant software and fintech.
  > "Bazaar restructured its operations following the 2022-2023 Pakistani rupee crash, shuttering capital-heavy warehouses and acquiring Keenu in July 2025 to scale digital merchant payments."
  [[benchmark-bazaar-technologies-pakistan]]

### Atomic Item: Jumbotail (India)
- Series D unicorn and profitable digital marketplace for Kirana stores.
  > "Jumbotail achieved full-year net profitability in FY25, raised a Series D round in June 2025 backed by SC Ventures, and acquired Solv India's FMCG assets, operating an asset-light fulfillment network with full fintech integration."
  [[benchmark-jumbotail-india]]

### Atomic Item: Chari (Morocco)
- Central Bank Payment Institution license and micro-credit integration.
  > "In October 2025, Bank Al-Maghrib granted Chari a formal Payment Institution license, enabling it to disburse credit lines directly to 25,000+ traditional hanouts with zero balance sheet default risk."
  [[benchmark-chari-morocco]]

## 3. Structural Thematic Dimensions

### Atomic Item: Asset-Light vs 1P Economics
- Structural unit economics inversion in 1P FMCG distribution.
  > "In branded FMCG distribution, gross margins are 3-5%. Operating owned warehouses and fleets costs 8-12% of GMV. The resulting -5% operating loss expands with volume, making scale fatal."
  [[analysis-asset-light-vs-1p]]

### Atomic Item: Anti-Disintermediation Mechanics
- The 4 pillars of anti-bypass defensibility.
  > "Platforms that rely solely on price comparison suffer 60%+ platform leakage within 6 months. Platforms that combine multi-vendor basket aggregation, embedded bank credit, and wholesaler ERP software reduce leakage to under 4%."
  [[analysis-anti-disintermediation-moats]]

### Atomic Item: Embedded Credit & Cheque Sayadi in Iran
- Tripartite credit architecture isolating platform balance sheet.
  > "By integrating with the Central Bank of Iran's Sayadi purple cheque clearing registry, POL enables commercial banks to underwrite working capital lines while maintaining zero balance-sheet default risk for POL."
  [[analysis-embedded-finance-credit-underwriting]]

### Atomic Item: Dynamic Clustered Cart Allocation
- Optimization of basket fill-rate versus delivery drops.
  > "Allowing unlimited multi-vendor splits causes store drop congestion; enforcing a single vendor causes 45% cart abandonment. Dynamic clustered routing with a strict cap of 2 fulfillment drops per order maximizes both fill rate and store compliance."
  [[pol-cart-allocation-spec]]
