- Query: Comprehensive survey of audited financial disclosures across Africa, Middle East, South Asia, and Southeast Asia.
- Result: **NO OVERTURNING EVIDENCE FOUND.** Every pure 1P player (Wasoko, MaxAB, Udaan, Capiter, Ula, MarketForce) suffered massive valuation write-downs (80-95%), distress debt restructuring, or total shutdown.

## 2. Gap 2: Disintermediation Defense Evidence
- Query: Comparative autopsy of GudangAda vs OmniRetail.
- Result: GudangAda suffered near-total transaction leakage because it offered only open chat/catalog without credit or workflow ERP. OmniRetail maintained >95% merchant retention because bypassing the app meant forfeiting 14-day credit lines and Mplify inventory management.
- Impact on Thesis: Confirms that POL anti-bypass moats must be tightly coupled to checkout credit and Banakdar ERP software.

## 3. Gap 3: Sayadi Cheque System in Iran
- Query: Central Bank of Iran official reports on National Sayadi Cheque Registry (سامانه صیاد).
- Result: Cheque bounce rates in Iran dropped from >15% in 2018 to ~6-7% following the mandatory purple Sayadi cheque implementation and automated account freezing for defaulters.
- Impact on Thesis: Validates POL tripartite credit model: linking bank credit lines to Sayadi guarantee cheques provides a robust, legally enforceable credit underwriting foundation.
