# Benchmark Deep Dive: ElasticRun (India)

## 1. Executive Summary & Identity
- **Company Name:** ElasticRun (NTEx Transportation Services)
- **Country:** India (rural and semi-urban reach covering 80,000+ villages and small towns across 28 states).
- **Founders:** Sandep Deshmukh, Saurabh Nigam, Shitiz Bansal.
- **Founded:** 2016.
- **Current Status (as of 2026-09-03):** Active unicorn. Raised $430M+ from SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Prosus Ventures, Goldman Sachs, and Kalaari Capital. Valued at $1.5 Billion.
- **Scale:** Connects top-tier FMCG brands (Hindustan Unilever, ITC, Marico, Britannia, P&G, Nestlé) directly to over 1 million rural kirana stores.

## 2. The Core Innovation: "Variable Capacity" Asset-Light Logistics
Rural distribution in emerging markets is notoriously unprofitable for brands: low order values, terrible road infrastructure, and dispersed demand.
- **The Crowdsourced Fleet Engine:** ElasticRun does not own trucks or employ full-time drivers. It created a tech platform that aggregates **idle commercial vehicle capacity** from local rural entrepreneurs, freight operators, and micro-transporters.
- **Dynamic Routing & Co-Loading:** Software pools FMCG deliveries to rural stores alongside e-commerce parcel deliveries (partnering with Amazon, Flipkart), ensuring trucks never travel half-empty.

## 3. Brand Direct Integration
Global FMCG brands struggled to reach beyond Tier-1/Tier-2 Indian cities without adding layers of rural wholesalers who extracted 8-12% margins. ElasticRun acts as the **direct digital distribution rail** for brands, providing them with real-time SKU tracking and pricing enforcement down to remote village shops.

## 4. Strategic Relevance to POL
- **Connecting Province Capital Wholesalers to Peripheral Towns:** In Iran, the provincial capitals (Shiraz, Tabriz, Mashhad, Isfahan) have large wholesale hubs, but peripheral towns and villages suffer high distribution markups. An ElasticRun-style variable logistics network can link metropolitan wholesalers to regional town supermarkets without buying trucks.
- **Enterprise Brand Monetization:** FMCG manufacturers in Iran (کاله، میهن، پاک، گلرنگ، چی توز) will gladly pay a digital rail that gives them verified reach into underserved store networks without distributor leakage.
