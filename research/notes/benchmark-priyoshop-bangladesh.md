# Benchmark Deep Dive: PriyoShop (Bangladesh)

## 1. Executive Summary & Identity
- **Company Name:** PriyoShop
- **Country:** Bangladesh (Dhaka, Chittagong, regional trading hubs)
- **Founder:** Asikul Alam Khan (Founder & CEO)
- **Founded:** 2013 as a B2C e-commerce platform; executed a complete **strategic pivot in July 2021 to an asset-light B2B marketplace for MSMEs**.
- **Current Status (as of 2026-09-03):** Active, capital-efficient survivor. Operating independently following the Sary-ShopUp merger in 2025. Focused strictly on high-margin unit economics, digital credit scoring, and software-enabled distribution.
- **Scale:** Tens of thousands of retail MSMEs, hundreds of supplier brands.

## 2. Why the Asset-Light Model Succeeded
- **Rejection of 1P Warehouses:** Having burned capital in the B2C era on inventory and fulfillment centers, Asikul Alam Khan designed the B2B model to be 100% asset-light. PriyoShop does not own warehouses or delivery vans.
- **Supplier-Direct Logistics:** Partners directly with FMCG suppliers and large wholesalers who fulfill orders using their existing logistical routes, coordinated by PriyoShop's dispatching software.
- **Focus on High-Margin SKU Bundles:** Prioritized high-margin non-perishable packaged goods, personal care, and confectionery rather than ultra-low-margin bulk commodities (raw grains), securing a higher blended take rate (3.5% - 5.0%).

## 3. Strategic Relevance to POL
- **Pivot Wisdom:** Proves that an asset-light architecture allows a tech startup to survive intense market downturns that crush asset-heavy giants.
- **Category Selection Discipline:** Demonstrates that curating the right product mix (high velocity + decent margin) is essential for marketplace sustainability.
