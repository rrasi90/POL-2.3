# Benchmark Deep Dive: Sary (Saudi Arabia) & SILQ Group

## 1. Executive Summary & Identity
- **Company Name:** Sary (merged into **SILQ Group** in April 2025).
- **Country:** Saudi Arabia (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, covering 15+ cities).
- **Founders:** Mohammed Aldossary (CEO, ex-Careem GM), Khaled Alsiari.
- **Founded:** 2018.
- **Current Status (as of 2026-09-03):** Active, major regional consolidator. Raised $112M+ from Sanabil Investments (PIF sovereign vehicle), Rocketship.vc, Raed Ventures, and VentureSouq. In 2022, acquired Egyptian B2B platform **Mowgli**. In **April 2025, executed a landmark cross-regional merger with Bangladesh's ShopUp to form SILQ Group**, creating an inter-regional trade finance and digital procurement corridor connecting GCC capital and commodities with South Asian retail demand.
- **Scale:** 40,000+ mini-markets (baqalas), restaurants, and catering businesses in Saudi Arabia; hundreds of thousands through the SILQ network.

## 2. Evolution: From FMCG Delivery to Trade Finance Infrastructure
- **Phase 1 (2018-2021): Domestic FMCG B2B Marketplace:** Launched as a mobile procurement platform for Saudi baqalas, reducing replenishment cycle time from days to under 3 hours in Riyadh.
- **Phase 2 (2022-2024): Enterprise Supply Chain & M&A:** Acquired Mowgli to expand into North Africa; integrated with wholesale distributors and manufacturers via automated ERP gateways.
- **Phase 3 (2025-2026): The SILQ Group Transformation:** Recognized that domestic FMCG margins in Saudi Arabia face structural limits due to modern supermarket penetration (Panda, Othaim, Carrefour). Shifted strategic focus to cross-border commodity procurement (rice, sugar, oil from South Asia to the Gulf) and embedded B2B supply chain financing backed by Saudi institutional capital.

## 3. Strategic Relevance to POL
- **The Limit of Domestic Pure-Play FMCG:** Even in high-income Saudi Arabia, domestic FMCG marketplace margins plateau at 3-4%. Long-term enterprise value comes from supply chain financing and cross-border trade.
- **Careem Alumni DNA:** Like Careem, Sary mastered localized operations and logistics tech before expanding.
- **Consolidation as an Exit/Scale Path:** Sary's merger with ShopUp proves that B2B platforms eventually aggregate into regional trade and financial infrastructure networks.
