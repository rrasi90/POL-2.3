## 1. Brand Hierarchy & Philosophy

POL (پُل) operates under a unified master-brand architecture. The retail-facing product and wholesale-facing product are **not separate corporate entities or divergent platforms**, but specialized product interfaces tailored for distinct actors in the B2B FMCG supply chain.

```
                         POL / پُل
                  (Corporate / Master Brand)
                             │
            ┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
            │                                 │
       POL Market                         POL Vendor
      پل مغازهدار                         پل بنکدار
   (Retailer Product)                 (Vendor Product)
```

### Brand Entities:
1. **Master / Corporate Identity:** **`POL`** (English) / **`پُل`** (Persian). Represents the technology platform, corporate identity, legal entity, investor relations, and trust infrastructure.
2. **Retailer-Facing Product:** **`POL Market`** (English) / **`پل مغازهدار`** (Persian). The procurement application, web ordering portal, and assisted-ordering interface for supermarket owners and grocery buyers.
3. **Vendor-Facing Product:** **`POL Vendor`** (English) / **`پل بنکدار`** (Persian). The warehouse management, catalog scanning, order fulfillment, dispatch, and settlement portal for FMCG wholesalers in Ahvaz Wholesalers Hub (شهرک بنکداران اهواز).

## 3. Surface & Touchpoint Usage Guidelines

To ensure brand cohesion and prevent user confusion, the three brand marks must never be mixed or interchanged:

| Touchpoint / Surface | POL Corporate (`پُل`) | POL Market (`پل مغازهدار`) | POL Vendor (`پل بنکدار`) | Strict Rule |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| **Mobile App Icon** | ❌ | ✅ (Retailer App) | ✅ (Vendor App) | Separate app launcher icons |
| **Splash Screen** | ❌ (Powered by POL mark allowed) | ✅ Main Splash | ✅ Main Splash | Product identity front & center |
| **Login / Auth Screen** | ❌ | ✅ Header | ✅ Header | Clear identity prevents wrong credential attempts |
| **Navbar / App Header** | ❌ | ✅ Top Left | ✅ Top Left | Persistent product branding |
| **Transactional SMS / Push** | ❌ (Mentioned as "پُل") | ✅ "پُل مغازه‌دار" | ✅ "پُل بنکدار" | SMS header must specify product context |
| **Email Communications** | ✅ Corporate Footer | ✅ Customer Service | ✅ Operations / Finance | Sender name matches persona |
| **Consolidated Invoices** | ✅ Master Header ("فاکتور پلتفرم پُل") | Subtotal Context | Child Order Detail | Official fiscal entity is POL |
| **Support UI / Help Desk** | ❌ | ✅ Retailer Chat | ✅ Vendor Support | Separate ticketing queues |
| **Investor Decks & Reports**| ✅ Primary | Mentioned as Product | Mentioned as Product | Unified investment vehicle |
| **Internal Admin Portal** | ✅ Primary Header | Managed Section | Managed Section | Platform operators see master brand |

## 4. Naming Standardization Rules (English & Persian)

### Canonical Names (Case-Sensitive & Spacing Standards):
- **Corporate:** `POL` (All uppercase in Latin) | `پُل` (Persian, with damma when necessary for clarity).
- **Retailer Product:** `POL Market` | `پل مغازهدار` (or `پُل مغازه‌دار` with standard half-space).
- **Vendor Product:** `POL Vendor` | `پل بنکدار` (or `پُل بنکدار` with standard half-space).

### Forbidden Variations in Active Documentation:
- ❌ Do NOT use "POL Retail", "POL Shop", "POL Store", or "سوپرمارکت پُل". The canonical retailer product name is **POL Market / پل مغازهدار**.
- ❌ Do NOT use "POL Wholesaler", "POL Supplier", or "تأمین‌کننده پُل". The canonical vendor product name is **POL Vendor / پل بنکدار**.
- ❌ Do NOT treat these entities as independent companies; they are specialized interfaces of the same POL platform.
